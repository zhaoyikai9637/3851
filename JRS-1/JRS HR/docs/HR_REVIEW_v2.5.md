# Manual HR resume review

Education, field of study, experience and graduate eligibility are written in
Job Requirements. HR reviews the resume and decides; missing legacy academic
fields cannot automatically reject or block an application.

Receive Application and Edit Contact Details do not require education or
graduation fields. Original candidate data and audit history remain. Job/stage,
booking-conflict, feedback-time, offer-approval and retention rules still apply.

For the current single-confirmation booking and removed shortcuts, see
[SIMPLE_WORKFLOW_v2.6.md](SIMPLE_WORKFLOW_v2.6.md).
