# Written job requirements

Each job stores 8 to 12 distinct substantive requirement lines. HR can select a
role template or write the requirements; normalization removes list numbering
and rejects empty, duplicate or excessive entries. Job metadata such as location
or department must not be inserted to pad the requirement count.

Saved requirements are shown in full next to the actual resume. Academic and
experience criteria are assessed manually. See HR_REVIEW_v2.5.md.
